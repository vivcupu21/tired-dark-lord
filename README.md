A 102-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses, and the small deflections — a hero kept waiting at the door, a step that never got fixed, a cup of tea gone cold — never in a flat announcement. The speaker is still a villain: thirty years of waiting for one eclipse, a tithe of souls, and the drowned gods bound to his will.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies the monologue in [`entry.txt`](entry.txt) byte-identical to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. The monologue is 102 words, 521 characters, UTF-8.
