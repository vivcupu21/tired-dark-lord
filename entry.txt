Come in. Mind the — the step. Third one. I had it fixed... twice.

You want the crown, the throne? Take a number. The queue starts at the siege of Aldermere, and I have stood in this cold since your grandfather was in short trousers.

Tonight the eclipse opens the river-gates, and my tithe of souls — yes, souls — binds the drowned gods to my will. Thirty years I waited for this sky.

Kneel, or fight, or — sit, actually. Sit. The chair by the fire is warm. The tea is... it was warm. Drink. We can do the dying after.
